Crispy Springs Community Guide

Welcome to Crispy Springs, a feature-rich community survival server built around exploration, cooperation, meaningful progression and a player-driven economy[cite: 11].

Crispy Springs supports both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition players[cite: 11]. You can progress through 550 levels, choose Jobs, build and protect a home, trade with other players, discover hidden treasures, complete achievements, choose whether to participate in optional PvP and create permanent Masterwork equipment through the Community Forge[cite: 11].

Start here

  • Complete the first-join Pathfinder journey[cite: 11].
  • Read and accept the server rules[cite: 11].
  • Collect your starter equipment[cite: 11].
  • Enter Survival and use /rtp to begin exploring[cite: 11].
  • Choose your Jobs and complete daily quests[cite: 11].
  • Protect your first home with a claim[cite: 11].
  • Browse player buy requests with /orders[cite: 11].
  • Apply for the Creator Programme if you make livestreams or gaming content[cite: 11].
  • Use /menu whenever you are unsure what to do next[cite: 11].

Important links

Getting Started

Joining the server

Crispy Springs supports Java and Bedrock cross-play through Geyser and Floodgate[cite: 12].

  • Server address: 147.135.6.160[cite: 12]
  • Port: 25899[cite: 12]

Bedrock usernames may display with a leading . in some server systems[cite: 12]. This is normal and keeps Java and Bedrock accounts separate[cite: 12].

Your first join

New players follow the Pathfinder onboarding journey around Spawn[cite: 12]. The journey introduces the server in this order[cite: 12]:

  1. Pathfinder[cite: 12]
  2. Archivist[cite: 12]
  3. Forgekeeper[cite: 12]
  4. Pathfinder[cite: 12]
  5. Guild Master[cite: 12]
  6. Claim Keeper[cite: 12]
  7. Pathfinder[cite: 12]

Starter equipment

The starter set includes an iron pickaxe, shovel and sword with improved starter enchantments[cite: 12]. These tools are unbreakable and bound to their owner[cite: 12]. The starter golden shovel is used for creating land claims[cite: 12].

The protected Forge starter set is issued through /forgestarter[cite: 12]. This is intended for onboarding or staff-directed recovery and will not create duplicate owned equipment[cite: 12].

Progression and Ranks

Crispy Springs uses a 550-level progression system[cite: 14]. Progression gives every player long-term goals while keeping staff and supporter status separate from gameplay rank[cite: 14].

RankLevels
Settler1–50[cite: 14]
Resident51–100[cite: 14]

Adventurer176–275[cite: 14]
Veteran276–400[cite: 14]
Legend401–550[cite: 14]

Use /progress to view your current task[cite: 14]. Nether access is granted automatically as the player reaches Overall Level 15 by completing Level 14[cite: 14].

Worlds and Travel

Spawn: Protected community hub with onboarding NPCs, Community Forge, and server links[cite: 15].

Survival: Permanent build world with a 1,000,000 × 1,000,000 block playable area[cite: 15].

Resource World: Accessible via /rw tp for gathering materials[cite: 15]. Do not build permanent bases here as it regenerates[cite: 15].

Nether: Unlocks upon reaching Overall Level 15[cite: 15].

Economy and Player Trading

Crispy Springs uses a player-driven economy[cite: 16]. Players earn money through Jobs, daily quests, achievements, trading, QuickShops, Auction House (/ah), and buy orders (/orders)[cite: 16].

Diamonds, emeralds, and ancient debris are protected from easy server sell loops to maintain market integrity[cite: 16].

Jobs

Jobs provide money and experience for normal gameplay activities[cite: 17]. Open the Jobs browser using /jobs browse[cite: 17].

Upcoming supporter subscriptions will increase active Job allowances and maximum Job levels (Supporter: +2/lvl 130, Supporter+: +3/lvl 160, Champion: +4/lvl 200)[cite: 17].

mcMMO Skills and Abilities

mcMMO adds RPG-style skill progression to Minecraft actions[cite: 18]. Use /mcstats to view your skill levels[cite: 18].

Store Global Boosters are planned to provide 1.5× mcMMO XP and 1.5× Jobs XP for three hours[cite: 18].

Claims and Build Protection

GriefPrevention claims protect homes, storage, and builds[cite: 19]. Use the golden shovel supplied during onboarding to claim territory[cite: 19].

Claims automatically extend downwards as you mine deeper[cite: 19]. PvP is strictly disabled inside all claims regardless of personal settings[cite: 19].
