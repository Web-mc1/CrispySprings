Crispy Springs Community Guide

Welcome to Crispy Springs, a feature-rich community survival server built around exploration, cooperation, meaningful progression and a player-driven economy[cite: 11].

Crispy Springs supports both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition players[cite: 11]. You can progress through 550 levels, choose Jobs, build and protect a home, trade with other players, discover hidden treasures, complete achievements, choose whether to participate in optional PvP and create permanent Masterwork equipment through the Community Forge[cite: 11].

Start here

  • Complete the first-join Pathfinder journey[cite: 11].
  • Read and accept the server rules[cite: 11].
  • Collect your starter equipment[cite: 11].
  • Enter Survival and use /rtp to begin exploring[cite: 11].
  • Choose your Jobs and complete daily quests[cite: 11].
  • Protect your first home with a claim[cite: 11].
  • Browse player buy requests with /orders[cite: 11].
  • Apply for the Creator Programme if you make livestreams or gaming content[cite: 11, 31].
  • Use /menu whenever you are unsure what to do next[cite: 11].

Important links

  • Store: crispy-springs.tebex.store
  • Discord: discord.gg/9K9r9kjSR4[cite: 11]

Getting Started

Joining the server

Crispy Springs supports Java and Bedrock cross-play through Geyser and Floodgate[cite: 12].

  • Server address: 147.135.6.160[cite: 12]
  • Port: 25899[cite: 12]

Bedrock usernames may display with a leading . in some server systems[cite: 12]. This is normal and keeps Java and Bedrock accounts separate[cite: 12].

Your first join

New players follow the Pathfinder onboarding journey around Spawn[cite: 12]. The journey introduces the server in this order[cite: 12]:

  1. Pathfinder[cite: 12]
  2. Archivist[cite: 12]
  3. Forgekeeper[cite: 12]
  4. Pathfinder[cite: 12]
  5. Guild Master[cite: 12]
  6. Claim Keeper[cite: 12]
  7. Pathfinder[cite: 12]

Starter equipment

The starter set includes an iron pickaxe, shovel and sword with improved starter enchantments[cite: 12]. These tools are unbreakable and bound to their owner[cite: 12]. The starter golden shovel is used for creating land claims[cite: 12].

The protected Forge starter set is issued through /forgestarter[cite: 12]. This is intended for onboarding or staff-directed recovery and will not create duplicate owned equipment[cite: 12].

Progression and Ranks

Crispy Springs uses a 550-level progression system[cite: 14]. Progression gives every player long-term goals while keeping staff and supporter status separate from gameplay rank[cite: 14].

Use /progress to view your current task[cite: 14]. Nether access is granted automatically as the player reaches Overall Level 15 by completing Level 14[cite: 14].

Worlds and Travel

Spawn: Protected community hub with onboarding NPCs, Community Forge, and server links[cite: 15].

Survival: Permanent build world with a 1,000,000 × 1,000,000 block playable area[cite: 15].

Resource World: Accessible via /rw tp for gathering materials[cite: 15]. Do not build permanent bases here as it regenerates[cite: 15].

Nether: Unlocks upon reaching Overall Level 15[cite: 15].

Economy and Player Trading

Crispy Springs uses a player-driven economy[cite: 16]. Players earn money through Jobs, daily quests, achievements, trading, QuickShops, Auction House (/ah), and buy orders (/orders)[cite: 16].

Diamonds, emeralds, and ancient debris are protected from easy server sell loops to maintain market integrity[cite: 16].

Jobs

Jobs provide money and experience for normal gameplay activities[cite: 17]. Open the Jobs browser using /jobs browse[cite: 17].

Upcoming supporter subscriptions will increase active Job allowances and maximum Job levels (Supporter: +2/lvl 130, Supporter+: +3/lvl 160, Champion: +4/lvl 200)[cite: 17].

mcMMO Skills and Abilities

mcMMO adds RPG-style skill progression to Minecraft actions[cite: 18]. Use /mcstats to view your skill levels[cite: 18].

Store Global Boosters are planned to provide 1.5× mcMMO XP and 1.5× Jobs XP for three hours[cite: 18].

Claims and Build Protection

GriefPrevention claims protect homes, storage, and builds[cite: 19]. Use the golden shovel supplied during onboarding to claim territory[cite: 19].

Claims automatically extend downwards as you mine deeper[cite: 19]. PvP is strictly disabled inside all claims regardless of personal settings[cite: 19].

PlayerWarps

PlayerWarps allow players to publish useful destinations such as towns, public farms, attractions and shops[cite: 21].

Normal warp limits come from progression[cite: 21]. Coming-soon Supporter subscriptions add additional warps (+2 for Supporter, +4 for Supporter+, +6 for Champion)[cite: 21]. Approved Creators receive one additional PlayerWarp[cite: 21].

Warp owners are responsible for keeping public destinations safe, accessible, and accurately described[cite: 21]. Traps or misleading commercial warps are not permitted[cite: 21].

Daily Quests and Achievements

Daily Quests

Players receive Easy, Medium, and Hard daily objectives that reset at 4:00 AM Pacific/Auckland time[cite: 22].

Selected Forge contracts award Equipment Gem Fragments[cite: 22]:

  • Easy Forge contract: 1 fragment[cite: 22]
  • Medium Forge contract: 2 fragments[cite: 22]
  • Hard Forge contract: 3 fragments[cite: 22]

Achievements

Use /achievements to browse achievement categories[cite: 22]. When a tooltip displays OBJECTIVE COMPLETE — CLICK TO CLAIM, click it to receive rewards like money, Community Coins, or XP boosters[cite: 22].

Community Treasures

Hidden treasures are placed around Spawn for players to discover[cite: 23]. Treasures use custom decorative heads rather than ordinary chests[cite: 23].

There are approximately 100 discoveries across Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Legendary designs contributing to a 24-stage Treasure Achievement track[cite: 23]. Completing the final stage unlocks the Seeker recognition[cite: 23].

Each account can receive credit for an individual treasure only once. Do not break decorative treasure heads[cite: 23].

Community Forge

The Community Forge allows players to craft, upgrade, socket, extract, and repair owned Masterwork tools, weapons, and armour[cite: 24]. Use a physical Community Forge Station or /forge as a menu backup[cite: 24].

Masterwork equipment is strictly bound to its owner[cite: 24]. Lost eligible items can be recovered through /forgereturn or /forgereturnitem[cite: 24].

Equipment Gems, Fragments and Cores

Equipment Gems add contextual bonuses to Masterwork equipment[cite: 25]. 9 genuine Equipment Gem Fragments craft one selected Gem I[cite: 25]. Equipment Gem Fragments do not drop from mob kills; obtain them through ores, Forge contracts, or treasure milestones[cite: 25].

Gem Stacking

Matching Gems stack with controlled diminishing returns[cite: 25]:

  • Strongest copy: 100% contribution[cite: 25]
  • Second copy: 50% contribution[cite: 25]
  • Third copy: 25% contribution[cite: 25]
  • Fourth+ copy: No additional effect[cite: 25]

Use /armourgems inspect to view active gem totals and diminishing returns[cite: 13, 25].

Graves and Death Recovery

AxGraves protects dropped inventory after death by creating a temporary grave[cite: 26]. Return to the grave location and interact with it to recover your items[cite: 26].

  • Read grave location and expiry details in chat immediately upon dying[cite: 26].
  • Ensure inventory space is available when retrieving items[cite: 26].
  • Forge ownership protections still apply to bound equipment[cite: 26].

Rules and Getting Help

Core Expectations

  • Treat other players and staff respectfully[cite: 28].
  • Do not grief, steal, or bypass land protection[cite: 28].
  • Do not use cheats, hacked clients, exploits, macros, or unattended automation[cite: 28].
  • Do not scam players or misrepresent trades[cite: 28].

If you need assistance, report issues on our Discord Support Channel[cite: 28].

Marketplace Buy Orders

DonutOrders lets players advertise what they want to buy instead of waiting for item listings[cite: 29]. Use /orders to open the marketplace interface[cite: 29].

The system supports 1,425 normal survival materials[cite: 29]. Spawn eggs, admin items, technical blocks, and PLAYER_HEAD are excluded[cite: 29].

Player versus Player (PvP)

Crispy Springs uses optional PvP[cite: 30]. Use /pvptoggle to toggle your personal combat setting[cite: 30].

PvP is strictly disabled inside player claims and protected regions like Spawn, overriding individual player settings[cite: 30]. Never use PvP to harass or repeatedly target players[cite: 30].

Crispy Springs Creator Programme

The Creator Programme recognises livestreamers and video creators who share Crispy Springs with their communities[cite: 31]. Creator is a secondary status: members continue progressing normally from Settler through Legend[cite: 31].

Creator Identity

Approved members display a Creator badge beside their normal player identity[cite: 31]:

[Explorer] PlayerName [✦ Creator]

While streaming, the badge changes automatically after the creator activates their verified stream link[cite: 31]:

[Explorer] PlayerName [● LIVE ✦ Creator]

Included Benefits

  • Creator and LIVE identification[cite: 31]
  • Controlled server-wide stream promotion[cite: 31]
  • Creator profile and approved platform links in the website directory[cite: 31]
  • Creator role and collaboration areas in Discord[cite: 31]
  • Opportunities to propose or host community events with staff support[cite: 31]
  • Early previews of selected upcoming server features[cite: 31]
  • One additional home (stacks with progression and subscriptions)[cite: 31]
  • One additional PlayerWarp (stacks with progression and subscriptions)[cite: 31]
  • Portable crafting with /craft[cite: 31]

Going LIVE

After staff verify and approve the creator's HTTPS Twitch, YouTube, or TikTok channel link, the creator can use /creatorlive on[cite: 31]. Activations grant a LIVE badge for up to six hours and send a server-wide announcement (subject to a 6-hour announcement cooldown)[cite: 31]. Use /creatorlive status to check remaining time and /creatorlive off when ending a stream[cite: 31].

Applying

Contact the Crispy Springs team through Discord and provide your Minecraft name, platform, channel link, usual content, and approximate streaming schedule[cite: 31].
