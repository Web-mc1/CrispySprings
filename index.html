Crispy Springs Community Guide

Welcome to Crispy Springs, a feature-rich community survival server built around exploration, cooperation, meaningful progression and a player-driven economy.[cite: 3]

Crispy Springs supports both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition players.[cite: 3] You can progress through 550 levels, choose Jobs, build and protect a home, trade with other players, discover hidden treasures, complete achievements and create permanent Masterwork equipment through the Community Forge.[cite: 3]

Start here

  • Complete the first-join Pathfinder journey.[cite: 3]
  • Read and accept the server rules.[cite: 3]
  • Collect your starter equipment.[cite: 3]
  • Enter Survival and use /rtp to begin exploring.[cite: 3]
  • Choose your Jobs and complete daily quests.[cite: 3]
  • Protect your first home with a claim.[cite: 3]
  • Use /menu whenever you are unsure what to do next.[cite: 3]

Getting Started

Joining the server

Starter equipment

The starter set includes an iron pickaxe, shovel and sword with improved starter enchantments.[cite: 3] These tools are unbreakable and bound to their owner.[cite: 3] The starter golden shovel is used for creating land claims.[cite: 3]

Starter items may be stored in personal storage, including Ender Chests, personal vaults and shulker boxes.[cite: 3] Another player cannot use your bound equipment.[cite: 3] If your starter equipment is missing after completing onboarding, contact staff rather than repeatedly claiming replacements.[cite: 3]

Worlds and Travel

Spawn

Spawn is the protected community hub.[cite: 3] It contains onboarding NPCs, the Community Forge, server information and links to major systems.[cite: 3]

Survival

Survival is the permanent building world.[cite: 3] Use /rtp to find an undeveloped location, then create a claim before building permanently.[cite: 3]

The Survival border is designed as a 1,000,000 × 1,000,000 block playable area, extending 500,000 blocks from the centre in each direction.[cite: 3]

Survival Nether

The Nether unlocks at progression level 15.[cite: 3] Players below the required level receive a locked-access message rather than being transported through the portal.[cite: 3]

The End

The permanent Survival End is currently closed.[cite: 3] Players should receive a message explaining that the End will open at a future date.[cite: 3] A renewable End resource experience is planned so future players can fight the dragon and obtain End resources and Elytras without permanently exhausting the main End.[cite: 3]

Resource World

Use /rw tp to travel randomly into the Resource World.[cite: 3] This world is intended for gathering and may be regenerated.[cite: 3] Do not place permanent homes, towns or valuable builds there.[cite: 3]

Economy and Player Trading

Crispy Springs uses a player-driven economy.[cite: 3] The server provides ways to earn and spend money, but direct player trade, QuickShops and the Auction House are intended to remain important.[cite: 3]

Earning money

Players can earn money through:[cite: 3]

  • Jobs[cite: 3]
  • Daily quests[cite: 3]
  • Achievements and progression[cite: 3]
  • Treasure discoveries[cite: 3]
  • Events and playtime rewards[cite: 3]
  • Selling to other players[cite: 3]
  • QuickShops and the Auction House[cite: 3]
  • Limited server buyback through /sell[cite: 3]

Rare materials are intentionally focused on the player market rather than unlimited server selling.[cite: 3] Diamonds, emeralds and ancient debris are examples of materials protected from easy unlimited server sell loops.[cite: 3]

Jobs

Jobs provide money and experience for normal gameplay activities.[cite: 3] Browse the available choices and select Jobs that match the way you enjoy playing.[cite: 3]

Job limits

Your maximum number of active Jobs and maximum Job level can be affected by progression and supporter benefits.[cite: 3]

Jobs are designed as one of the main sources of income.[cite: 3] Automated or exploitative activity is not permitted.[cite: 3]

mcMMO Skills and Abilities

mcMMO adds long-term skill progression to familiar Minecraft activities.[cite: 3] As skills increase, passive bonuses and active abilities become stronger or last longer.[cite: 3]

Use /mcstats to view your levels and /mcmmo for general help.[cite: 3]

Boosters

Global mcMMO boosters may be activated through store packages or rewards.[cite: 3] The current global booster provides 1.5× mcMMO XP alongside 1.5× Jobs XP for three hours.[cite: 3] Global multiplier stacking is capped at 1.5×.[cite: 3]

Claims and Build Protection

GriefPrevention claims protect homes, storage and builds from unwanted changes.[cite: 3] Create a claim before leaving valuable items in Survival.[cite: 3]

Creating a claim

  1. Hold the golden shovel supplied during onboarding.[cite: 3]
  2. Select one corner of the area you want to protect.[cite: 3]
  3. Select the opposite corner.[cite: 3]
  4. Check that the claim outline covers the complete build.[cite: 3]

You earn claim blocks through play and selected rewards.[cite: 3] Only trust players you know.[cite: 3] Different trust levels may permit entry, container use or building.[cite: 3]

PlayerWarps allow players to publish useful destinations such as towns, public farms, attractions and shops.[cite: 3]

Daily Quests and Achievements

Daily quests

Players receive a mixture of Easy, Medium and Hard daily objectives.[cite: 3] Quests are selected per player and provide rewards for participating in different server activities.[cite: 3]

Daily quests reset at 4:00 AM Pacific/Auckland time.[cite: 3]

Achievements

Use /achievements to browse achievement categories, requirements and rewards.[cite: 3] When a tooltip displays OBJECTIVE COMPLETE — CLICK TO CLAIM, click the achievement to receive the reward and record it as claimed.[cite: 3]

Community Treasures

Hidden treasures are placed around Spawn for players to discover while exploring.[cite: 3] Treasures use custom decorative heads rather than ordinary loot chests.[cite: 3]

There are approximately 100 discoveries across Common, Uncommon, Rare and Legendary treasure designs.[cite: 3] Each discovery contributes to the 24-stage Treasure Achievement track.[cite: 3]

How treasures work

  1. Explore Spawn carefully.[cite: 3]
  2. Interact with a treasure you have not previously discovered.[cite: 3]
  3. Check the Treasure Achievements menu.[cite: 3]
  4. Claim any newly completed stage.[cite: 3]

Community Forge

The Community Forge is Crispy Springs’ permanent equipment progression system.[cite: 3] It allows players to craft, upgrade, socket, extract and repair owned Masterwork tools, weapons and armour.[cite: 3]

Equipment ownership

Masterwork equipment is bound to the player who owns it:[cite: 3]

  • Other players cannot use or collect it as their own.[cite: 3]
  • It may be stored in personal vaults, Ender Chests, shulker boxes and other permitted private storage.[cite: 3]
  • Lost eligible equipment can be recovered through the Forge return system.[cite: 3]

Upgrading equipment

A Masterwork upgrade requires the existing owned item in the designated equipment slot.[cite: 3] This prevents crafting a new item from accidentally replacing a higher-tier item.[cite: 3]

Equipment Gems, Fragments and Cores

Equipment Gems add specialised bonuses to Masterwork tools, weapons and armour.[cite: 3] Available sockets depend on the item and equipment tier.[cite: 3]

Gem progression

  • Fragments are used to craft Tier I gems.[cite: 3]
  • Compatible Tier I gems can be combined into Tier II.[cite: 3]
  • Tier II combined with Tier I creates Tier III where the recipe permits it.[cite: 3]
  • Socket tooltips show how many compatible sockets remain.[cite: 3]
  • Gems may be extracted and reused when the extraction requirements are met.[cite: 3]

Equipment Gem Fragments do not drop from mob kills.[cite: 3]

A complete Tier II armour set grants an additional 5% gem-stat set bonus.[cite: 3]

Graves and Death Recovery

AxGraves protects dropped inventory after death by creating a temporary grave.[cite: 3] Return to the grave and interact with it to recover your items.[cite: 3]

Important advice

  • Read the grave location and expiry information immediately.[cite: 3]
  • Do not delay recovery unnecessarily.[cite: 3]
  • Bring appropriate equipment when returning to a dangerous area.[cite: 3]
  • Make sure your inventory has room if the grave cannot restore everything automatically.[cite: 3]
  • Forge ownership protections still apply to bound equipment.[cite: 3]

Supporter Subscriptions

Supporter subscriptions help fund Crispy Springs while providing convenience, cosmetics and monthly rewards.[cite: 3] Benefits remain active only while subscribed and do not replace gameplay progression.[cite: 3]

Rules and Getting Help

Crispy Springs is built around community, creativity, fairness, exploration and meaningful progression.[cite: 3]

Core expectations

  • Treat other players and staff respectfully.[cite: 3]
  • Do not grief, steal or bypass land protection.[cite: 3]
  • Do not use cheats, hacked clients, exploits, macros or unattended automation.[cite: 3]
  • Do not scam players or deliberately misrepresent trades.[cite: 3]
  • Keep public builds, shops and PlayerWarps safe and appropriately named.[cite: 3]
  • Do not build permanent projects in renewable resource worlds.[cite: 3]
  • Report exploits privately rather than demonstrating them publicly.[cite: 3]
  • Follow staff directions and the complete rules accepted during onboarding.[cite: 3]
